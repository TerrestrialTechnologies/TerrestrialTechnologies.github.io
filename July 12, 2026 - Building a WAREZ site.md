# July 12, 2026 - Building a 2001 warez site. 

Tonight i wanted to put my hands on HTML and CSS. rather than read theory i thought it might be wise 
to build something i find enjoyment in. Well i was musing one night this past week about how much I
missed the sort of "wild-west" feel of the internet of the late 90s and early oughts--- where i did 
absolutely nothing illegal as an adolescent. 

  My goals in all of this were to create my first standalone HTML project. it's kind of barebones, 
now-- but i intend to add a lot more to it as i dig further down this rabbit hole. I thought to 
better get an idea of fleshing this out at the outset though, and perhaps to remind myself that 
this is going to be a multi-layer project. as i expect every project to be. I would be lying if 
i said one of my goals though, wasn't to put another green square on the git commit board. 

  What I think I learned from all of this was first- HTML structure. I familiarized myself with 
headings, parahraphs, horizontal rules, preformatted text, and marquee elements. As far as CSS, 
I was able to change text colors, center content (this one threw me for a loop, as simple as it
was,) and how to separate content from presentation- which I've read is important. 

## Final thoughts 

Building something taught me a lot more than just reading about it. and i think that matters. 
If anyone ever does read this, I implore you-- build something. It helps a lot more than you might
think. The website might be on the ridiculous side- but the lessons i learned were very much 
concrete. 

  My next steps will include adding javascript, creating more pages, building a fake login portal,
and continue HTML/CSS studies. along with whatever other languages catch my fancy.
